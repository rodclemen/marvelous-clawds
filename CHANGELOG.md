# What's new in Clawvengers: Clawd Wars

The changes you'd notice in the Clawvel universe, newest first. Clawvengers: Clawd Wars now lives on as part of [Clawd Clash](https://github.com/rodclemen/clawd-clash); its [changelog](https://github.com/rodclemen/clawd-clash/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) has the full story, every detail included.

## 3.0.0: joins Clawd Clash

- **One app, three universes.** Clawvengers: Clawd Wars is now the Clawvel universe of Clawd Clash, next to Star Claws and Detective Claws. Pick one in Settings; a scene never mixes them. Switching recasts everyone on screen and moves the scene to one of the new universe's places.
- **Parody names.** Everyone and everywhere in Clawvel has a new name: Iron Clawd, Captain Ameriklaw, Thorr, Spider-Clawd, Clawverine and Thanoz, Clawvengers Tower, Wakandu and Asgaard, the Clawjet, and the app itself is now Clawvengers: Clawd Wars.
- **Comic-book transitions.** Moving to a new place, in the window and the screen saver, now happens like a turn of the page: halftone dots, panels slamming down, a speed-line zoom, or a "MEANWHILE, IN…" caption. The sound effect belongs to someone on screen: SNIKT! claw slashes for Clawverine, a PHOOMF! of smoke for Nightclawler, KRA-KA-BOOM! lightning for Thorr, a FWIP! of web for Spider-Clawd, a BAROOM! for The Bulk, or a classic KAPOW!. Deadpond sometimes takes over, peeling the page off the screen or surfing past on a chimichanga.
- **Random universe.** Settings can switch universe on its own every 1 to 60 minutes. In the window, the whole thing changes over with the new universe's transition while the new crew walks in underneath.
- **A Settings window.** Every option in one place (⌘,): the universe, the window and the desktop, which AIs are connected, the screen saver. The menu is shorter for it.
- **The window steps aside.** When no AI is running, the window fades away instead of showing an empty scene, and comes back when one starts.
- **Keep Characters on Top**, or not: the desktop crew can now live on the desktop itself, behind your windows.
- **The screen saver picks its own universe** in its Options, Random included, and no longer goes missing from System Settings' list after an update.
- **Powers that look right.** Only the real lightning throwers (Stormy, Elektro, Wikkan) still crackle with it; Thanoz, Doctor Gloom, Dormamoo, Mefisto and the other casters fire a steady stream in their own colour, and Emma Frosty turns to diamond and fights hand to hand.
- **Upgrading takes care of itself.** Clawd Clash takes over the AIs the old app had connected, quits it and starts in Clawvel. The Homebrew cask `avengers-clawd-wars` now installs Clawd Clash.
- **A new icon**: a comic cover in three panels, one per universe, with Spider-Clawd hanging from his web in the Clawvel one.

## 0.1.0: the first Clawvel release

- **A Clawvel battle on your desktop.** Your Claude Code, Codex, Qwen Code, Gemini CLI and Copilot CLI sessions as pixel-art heroes and villains who fight while they work, in a window or along the bottom of your screen.
- **193 characters**, 105 heroes and 88 villains, every one Clawd in costume and nearly every one with a signature move: Iron Clawd flips his faceplate up, Thanoz snaps, Lokee turns into a snake, Deadpond eats a chimichanga.
- **Claude Code plays the heroes** whenever another AI is running; with Claude alone, anyone can turn up.
- **Everyone fights their own way**: shields knock shots aside, every shooter has its own shots, and magic comes in each caster's colours.
- **Arch-enemies face off first**: Captain Ameriklaw and Red Skulk, Thorr and Lokee, Spider-Clawd and the Green Gobbler, Clawverine and Sabreclaw, and more.
- **Flyers fly.** They take off and land as they cross the stage, now and then lift off for a while, and keep shooting from the air in a fight. Hoverers float, and Spider-Clawd swings on a web line.
- **The Clawjet and the Milano** land to pick up their crews when they're done.
- **43 places**, from Clawvengers Tower to Ta Loh, each with something moving, and the scene is set where the first session's character belongs.
- **A screen saver** with the place full screen, the crew, cards, name tags and a clock.
- **"Needs you" waits as long as it takes**: a session waiting for your answer keeps its red `!` until you answer, and stands down if you press Esc.
- **A trailer** with its own soundtrack, at the top of the README.
